Uko PV Africa Ikora

PV Africa ni umushinga digitale washinzwe na Emirete Zunze Ubumwe z'Abarabu(UAE), Leta Zunze Ubumwe z'Amerika(USA) n'umuryango w'Africa Yunze Ubumwe(UA) ugamije guha abanyamuryango serivisi zijyanye n'ishoramari ku masoko atandukanye hirya no hino ku isi.

Uko Ubucuruzi Bukora

1

Fungura Konti Yawe

Umunyamuryango mushya atangira afungura konti ya PV Africa akoresheje amakuru ye bwite. Nyuma yo kwiyandikisha, ashobora kugera kuri dashboard ye no kureba amakuru ya konti ye.

2

Kugira Konti Yawe Active

Abanyamuryango bagira konti zabo active hakurikijwe ibisabwa bya PV Africa byo kugira konti active aho utanga umusanzu wa 4,100 RWF. Umugabane uhisemo niwo ugena inyungu ya buri munsi umunyamuryango yunguka.

3

Koresha Amahirwe yo Kwinjiza Buri Munsi

Nyuma yo kugira konti active. abanyamuryango bashobora gukoresha uburyo bwo kwinjiza amafaranga buboneka kuri uru rubuga kandi bagakora ibikorwa bya buri munsi byemewe. Amafaranga yinjijwe ashobora kubikuzwa igihe cyose.

4

Kurikirana Konti Yawe

Dashboard ifasha abanyamuryango gukurikirana amafaranga bashoye, inyungu ya buri munsi, amafaranga yose bamaze kwinjiza, ibihembo, abo batumiye, amafaranga babikuje n'ibindi bikorwa bya konti.

5

Tumira Inshuti

Abanyamuryango bashobora gusangiza inshuti zabo imikorere ya PrimeVest Africa, uwatumiwe yiyandikishije kandi akuzuza ibisabwa, uwamutumiye ahabwa komisiyo ingana na 1,500 RWF.

6

Saba Kubikuza

Iyo amafaranga aboneka kuri konti y'umunyamuryango ageze ku kigero gisabwa kugira ngo abikuzwe(1,200 RWF), Umunyamuryango yohereza ubusabe bwo kubikuza akoresheje uburyo bwo kubikuza bwemewe(MTN,Airtel). Ubusabe bwo kubikuza bukurikiza amafaranga make asabwa(1,200 RWF), amafaranga ya serivisi n'amabwiriza yo kugenzura konti y'urubuga, Umunyamuryango amafaranga amugeraho mugihe kitarenze amasaha 24.

Ubucuruzi bwa PV Africa Buturuka He?

Uburyo Ubucuruzi Bwacu Bukora

PV Africa ikora nk'umushinga wa digitale uhuza abanyamuryango na serivisi ziboneka zo kwinjiza amafaranga ndetse n'izijyanye n'ishoramari. Urubuga rushobora kubona amafaranga y'ubucuruzi binyuze mu bikorwa byarwo by'ubucuruzi, ubufatanye, amasezerano ya serivisi n'andi masoko y'amafaranga yemewe.


Igice cy'amafaranga urubuga rwinjiza gishobora gukoreshwa mu bikorwa byo gukoresha urubuga, ikoranabuhanga, gufasha abakiriya, kwamamaza n'ibindi bisohoka mu bucuruzi. Iyo bikenewe, urubuga rukoresha amafaranga y'ubucuruzi mu gushyigikira ibihembo by'abanyamuryango na serivisi z'urubuga hakurikijwe amabwiriza ya buri gahunda iboneka.

Icyitonderwa: Ntibyemewe guha amakuru ya konti yawe buri wese uretse umuyobozi, ntiwemerewe gutanga ijambo banga ryawe.

Uko Abanyamuryango Bashobora Kwinjiza Amafaranga

Amafaranga Yinjizwa Buri Munsi

Abanyamuryango bujuje ibisabwa binjiza amafaranga binyuze mu bikorwa cyangwa imirimo iboneka buri munsi.

Ibihembo byo Gutumira

Tumira abanyamuryango bujuje ibisabwa maze ubone ibihembo bingana na 1,500 RWF kuri buri muntu watumiye.

Gahunda z'Ishoramari

Gera kuri gahunda z'ishoramari ziboneka kandi ukurikirane uko zikora ukoresheje konti yawe.

Kubikuza

Amafaranga aboneka kuri konti guhera ku 1,200 RWF arabikuzwa hakoreshejwe uburyo bwo kubikuza bwemewe(MTN,Airtel).

Ibyo Urubuga Rutanga

Dashboard Yawe

Reba amafaranga ufite, ayo winjije, amakuru y'ishoramari, ibihembo byo gutumira n'ibikorwa bya konti yawe ahantu hamwe.

Amafaranga Yinjizwa Buri Munsi

Kurikirana amafaranga winjiza buri munsi kandi urebe uko amafaranga aboneka kuri konti yawe agenda ahinduka uko igihe kigenda.

Gahunda yo Gutumira

Tumira inshuti kandi ukurikirane abo watumiye bujuje ibisabwa n'ibihembo byo gutumira kuri konti yawe.

Kubikuza

Ohereza ubusabe bwo kubikuza ukoresheje uburyo bwo kubikuza bwemewe kandi ukurikirane uko bigenda.

Gukurikirana Ibikorwa

Kurikirana ibikorwa by'ingenzi bya konti, birimo amafaranga winjije, ibihembo, amafaranga washyizemo n'ayo wakuye kuri konti.

Intego Yacu

Intego yacu ni ugutanga akazi kubakifuza mu buryo bw'ikorana buhanga kandi bwizewe bw'igihe kitari munsi y'imyaka 3 - 5.

Icyerekezo Cyacu

Icyerekezo cyacu ni guteza imbere abatuye isi muburyo bwa digitale bwizewe, bufite imikoreshereze igezweho, amakuru asobanutse, imikorere y'ubucuruzi ishinzwe neza n'ubufasha bwizewe ku banyamuryango.

Kuki Wahitamo PV Africa?

Umutekano wa Konti

Duharanira gutanga uburyo bwizewe bwo kugera kuri konti no kurinda amakuru y'abanyamuryango.

Imikoreshereze Yoroshye

Kuyobora urubuga mu buryo bworoshye bifasha abanyamuryango gucunga imari yabo vuba.

Umucyo

Abanyamuryango bashobora kubona amakuru y'ingenzi ya konti yabo kuri dashboard.

Ubufasha

Itsinda ryacu ry'ubufasha rirahari kugira ngo rifashe abanyamuryango ku bibazo bijyanye n'urubuga.

Twandikire & Ubufasha

Ukeneye ubufasha? Itsinda ryacu ry'ubufasha rishobora kugufasha ku bijyanye no kugera kuri konti, kuyikoresha, kubikuza, gutumira abandi n'ibibazo rusange by'urubuga.
